# ENDURAW — Readiness App

A daily readiness tracking app for endurance athletes. Measure your perception of physical and mental state through a quick daily check-in, powered by sport science.

## What It Does

- **Daily Check-in**: Answer 11 simple questions (~60 seconds) about your readiness state
- **Body Awareness**: Track 6 key dimensions: Energy, Recovery, Mental Availability, Physical Aptitude, Confidence, Ambition
- **Scientific Insights**: Compare your perception vs. objective data to understand your actual state
- **Predictive Analytics**: Get insights on how your readiness correlates with performance

## Why It Matters

EndurAW responds to three key athlete questions:
- *How do I feel today?*
- *What's my actual state of form?*
- *Can I trust my perception to predict future performance?*

## Tech Stack

- **Frontend**: React + TypeScript
- **Backend**: Python (data processing)
- **Database**: PostgreSQL
- **Deployment**: Vercel
